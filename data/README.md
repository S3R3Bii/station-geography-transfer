# Purpose-bound study inputs

Original official ZIP/CSV files stay private outside every Git repository. Only coarse derived station/day departure counts and rounded aggregate station geometry used by this noncommercial independent analysis are candidates for the reviewed study package. The source license is not a general public-domain license and does not authorize a standalone source-data mirror, customer linkage or implied endorsement.

Ride/customer/bike identifiers, rider types, ending positions, demographics, exact trip timestamps and individual trajectories are excluded from the analysis/public package. Missing whole files, unknown station keys, bad dates, coordinate-screen exclusions and zero published-record grid cells must be distinguished. Published record counts do not establish open stations, unserved demand or bike/dock availability.

Source schema, authorized archive requests, hashes and independent parse/aggregate checks accompany the derived facts. Original-vintage availability and the accuracy/completeness of provider records remain unverified.
